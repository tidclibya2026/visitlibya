Explore Libya

Journey through ancient cities, desert oases, Mediterranean shores, and the green landscapes of the east.

Legacy of Rome by the sea

Leptis Magna

Monumental architecture and ancient streets reveal Roman heritage in a striking Mediterranean setting.

History facing the Mediterranean

Sabratha

Its Roman theatre and sea-facing ruins shape an unforgettable coastal archaeological landscape.

The Pearl of the Desert

Ghadames

A UNESCO oasis city of traditional architecture, shaded passages, and enduring desert heritage.

The Sahara’s ancient story

Akakus

Rock formations, prehistoric art, and vast desert scenery preserve traces of early human memory.

Ancient wonders in the Green Mountain

Cyrene / Shahhat

Classical temples and ruins meet mountain landscapes at a site of great North African significance.

Heritage meets Mediterranean life

Tripoli Old City

Historic streets, traditional markets, and Ottoman and Mediterranean architecture express the capital’s living spirit.

Nature, mountains, and heritage

Jebel Akhdar / Green Mountain

Green valleys, forests, cliffs, and coastal beauty connect Shahat, Sousa, and Ras Al Hilal.

A desert oasis like no other

Ubari Lakes

Golden dunes frame desert lakes in one of the Sahara’s most memorable landscapes.

Urban Heritage

Tripoli Marcus Arch

A gateway into the historic memory of Tripoli’s old city.
