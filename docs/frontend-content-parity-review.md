# Frontend Content Parity Review

Status: Editorial review required. No factual content was changed in this batch.

## Source treatment

The Arabic pages are currently treated as the provisional richer source for **Experiences** and **Heritage** because they contain dedicated sections not represented in English. This is a working editorial observation, not an approval that every Arabic statement is accurate or final.

## Sections missing from English pages

### Experiences

The English page does not have dedicated equivalents for: Desert and Adventure; Safari; Camping; Coast and Sea; Marine and Equestrian Clubs; Horse Riding; Green Mountain; Festivals and Events; Wellness; Suggested Routes; Civilizations Route; Green Mountain Route; Desert Route; Oases Route; and Nafusa Mountains Route.

### Heritage

The English page has cards for Leptis Magna, Sabratha, Cyrene/Shahhat, Old Town of Ghadames, and the Rock-Art Sites of Tadrart Acacus, but lacks dedicated equivalents for: World Heritage overview; Rock Art; Old Cities; Underwater Heritage; Castles and Forts; Garamantian Civilization; Museums; and Traditional Markets.

### Culture

Editorial reconciliation is required rather than copying either version. The English page has dedicated themes for culture across generations, music/performance/horse traditions, handmade identity, civilizations in place, and several promotional feature panels. It lacks direct equivalents for the Arabic sections: Sweets; Contemporary Restaurants; Folklore and Festivals; Markets; and Hospitality. The Arabic page lacks direct equivalents for several English thematic panels and calls to action.

### Atlas

The English page lacks direct equivalents for the Arabic explanatory sections: What is the Atlas?; What does it contain?; How does it help the visitor?; and Tourism Layers. The English Filters for Trusted Discovery section has no direct Arabic equivalent.

### Plan

The English page lacks direct equivalents for the Arabic sections: Before Travel; Currency; Banks; Transportation; Safety; and Frequently Asked Questions. The Arabic page lacks direct equivalents for the English sections: Choose Your Theme; Pick Your Route; Prepare Responsibly; Build Your Trip; and Your Journey Begins in Libya.

### AI

Both pages provide a demonstration assistant, but their introductory copy, preset questions, and response coverage differ. Question sets and visitor guidance require bilingual editorial reconciliation before they are treated as equivalent.

## Required review

- Content-owner approval is required before copying, deleting, consolidating, or translating sections.
- Factual verification is required for UNESCO status and terminology, geography, heritage narratives, visa/entry rules, customs requirements, currency statements, fees, thresholds, and official links.
- Existing numerical customs thresholds elsewhere in the site were not changed. They require official verification before publication and must not be treated as current solely because they appear in source content.
- This batch changes metadata, accessibility text, and the approved neutral services wording only; it does not reconcile or rewrite Experiences, Heritage, Culture, Atlas, Plan, or AI body content.
