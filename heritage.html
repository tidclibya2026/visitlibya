UNESCO World Heritage

A Journey Through World Heritage

Libya’s UNESCO World Heritage sites reveal the depth of its civilization and its global significance across the Mediterranean and Sahara.

Roman urban legacy

Leptis Magna

Monumental public buildings and ancient streets preserve the scale and artistry of a great Roman city by the sea.

Classical mountain city

Cyrene / Shahhat

Temples, sanctuaries, and classical ruins express the historic importance of Cyrene in North Africa.

Theatre by the sea

Sabratha

A celebrated Roman theatre and archaeological city stand within a distinctive Mediterranean coastal landscape.

Prehistoric rock art

Rock-Art Sites of Tadrart Acacus

Ancient images across a dramatic Sahara landscape record environments, communities, and human life over millennia.

Historic oasis city

Old Town of Ghadames

Earthen architecture and covered passages reveal an ingenious urban tradition shaped by oasis life.
