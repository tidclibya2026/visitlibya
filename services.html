FAQ

Electronic Visa & Entry Requirements FAQ

Information is guidance only. Eligibility, fees, visa on arrival, and entry rules must be checked through official channels before travel.

Electronic Visa

How do I know if I am eligible to apply for the tourist eVisa?

Eligibility should be checked through the official Libya eVisa government portal. Travelers should enter their nationality, passport type, and purpose of visit to see the available options according to the platform rules.

Open Official Libya eVisa Portal
How much does the tourist eVisa cost?

Visa fees may vary by visa type, nationality, duration, and official procedures. Travelers should check the latest fee through the government eVisa portal during the application process.

Can I cancel my eVisa or get a refund if the application is rejected?

Cancellation and refund requests are subject to the official platform’s policy and competent authorities. Travelers should review the terms and fee policy before submitting and paying.

Do I need medical insurance to apply for a tourist visa?

Medical insurance requirements may vary by instructions and visa type. Travelers should check the official Libya eVisa portal and are advised to obtain travel and medical insurance covering their stay.

I hold a diplomatic or special passport. Can I apply for a tourist eVisa?

Diplomatic and special passports may be subject to different procedures. Travelers should verify through the official platform or competent authorities before applying.

Entry Requirements

Can I obtain a tourist visa on arrival in Libya?

Visa on arrival depends on rules that may vary by nationality, passport type, port of entry, and official instructions. Travelers should verify this through the official Libya eVisa portal before travel.

I have a Schengen, UK, or US visa. Can I obtain a tourist visa on arrival in Libya?

Holding a Schengen, UK, or US visa does not automatically grant entry to Libya or guarantee visa on arrival. Travelers should verify the current rules through the official Libya eVisa portal or competent authorities.

I have permanent residence in the UK, US, or EU. Can I obtain a tourist visa on arrival?

Permanent residence in another country does not automatically guarantee entry or visa on arrival in Libya. The official Libya eVisa portal and competent authorities are the reference.

My country is not listed as eligible for the tourist eVisa. How can I apply for a tourist visa?

If your country does not appear as eligible on the official eVisa portal, contact the Libyan embassy, consulate, or competent authority to confirm the official tourist visa procedure.

Can tourist visa holders enter Libya through land borders?

Land border entry depends on official instructions, the port status, and visa type. Travelers should verify the permitted entry points before travel.

Can I enter Libya as a tourist?

Tourism entry is possible when the traveler has the correct visa or entry authorization, a valid passport, and complies with official entry requirements at the time of travel.

General Information

Can I use a tourist visa to work in Libya?

No. A tourist visa is for tourism and visits only. Work requires the appropriate visa and permits from the competent authorities.

What is the national currency of Libya?

The national currency of Libya is the Libyan dinar, divided into 1,000 dirhams. Banknotes and coins are available for local transactions.

Do I need to declare currency when entering or leaving Libya?

Currency declaration requirements and permitted limits may change. Check the latest official customs guidance before travel.

Can international payment cards be used?

Card and electronic payment services may vary by city, establishment, and bank. Travelers should confirm available payment methods in advance and carry suitable cash for daily expenses.
