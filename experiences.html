Things to Do

Travel Through Things to Do, Not Only Places

From the Sahara to the coast, and from Roman cities to living culture, every journey tells a different Libya.

ExperienceDesert Adventures

Explore dunes, rock art, desert routes, camping, and guided discovery across Libya’s southern landscapes.

 ExperienceRoman Heritage

Walk through Leptis Magna, Sabratha, Cyrene, theatres, temples, arches, and ancient Mediterranean cities.

 ExperienceCoastal Escape

Discover Mediterranean beaches, coastal heritage cities, old ports, and peaceful sea views.

 ExperienceCultural Immersion

Experience local hospitality, traditional dress, crafts, markets, cuisine, and folklore.

 ExperienceJebel Akhdar / Green Mountain Nature

Explore valleys, forests, cliffs, beaches, and the natural beauty of Jebel Akhdar / Green Mountain.

 ExperienceFood & Markets

Taste Libyan cuisine and experience old markets, tea, spices, dishes, and local stories.
